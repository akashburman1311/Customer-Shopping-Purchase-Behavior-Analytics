# 🛍️ Customer Shopping & Purchase Behavior Analytics Dashboard

An interactive analytics dashboard designed to analyze customer shopping behavior, revenue performance, product sales, purchasing patterns, and customer preferences.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of customer and product-level performance through interactive filters, KPI cards, and visual analytics.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

This project contains two analytical dashboard pages:

### 1. Customer & Revenue Overview

This page provides an executive-level overview of customer activity and revenue performance.

#### Key KPIs
- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Average Purchase Value
- Average Review Rating
- Average Previous Purchases
- Subscriber Percentage
- Discount Users Percentage

#### Visualizations
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Season
- Customer Distribution by Gender
- Revenue by Age Group
- Top 10 Items by Revenue
- Purchase Frequency Distribution

#### Interactive Filters
- Category
- Season
- Gender
- Age Group
- Payment Method
- Subscription Status

---

### 2. Product & Purchase Behavior

This page focuses on product performance and customer purchasing behavior.

#### Key KPIs
- Total Transactions
- Total Purchase Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Total Products
- Best-Selling Category
- Best-Selling Items
- Average Customer Rating

#### Visualizations
- Top 10 Products by Purchase Amount
- Category vs Average Purchase Amount
- Item Purchases by Category
- Revenue by Size
- Revenue by Colour
- Average Rating by Category

#### Interactive Filters
- Category
- Season
- Gender
- Age Group
- Payment Method
- Subscription Status

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which items are the top revenue contributors?
- How does revenue vary across different seasons?
- Which age groups contribute the most revenue?
- What is the distribution of customers by gender?
- How frequently do customers make purchases?
- Which products have the highest purchase amounts?
- Which categories have the highest average purchase value?
- Which sizes and colours generate the most revenue?
- How do customer ratings vary across product categories?
- How does subscription status relate to customer purchasing behavior?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard highlights several important business metrics and patterns, including:

- Clothing is the highest-revenue category in the customer and revenue overview.
- The 56+ age group contributes the highest revenue among the displayed age groups.
- Customer purchasing frequency is distributed across weekly, monthly, quarterly, and other purchase intervals.
- The dashboard identifies the leading products based on purchase amount.
- Clothing, footwear, accessories, and outerwear can be compared based on average purchase amount and customer ratings.
- Product performance can be further analyzed using size, colour, category, season, gender, and subscription filters.
